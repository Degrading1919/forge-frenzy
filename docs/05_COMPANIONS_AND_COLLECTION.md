# Forge Companions and collection systems

## Why companions exist — PLANNED

Forge Companions provide the familiar pet-based secondary upgrade loop without replacing weapon forging as the central collection experience. Companions are cute magical or mechanical helpers with **specialized workshop bonuses**, not fighting pets. Players should see their favorites accompanying them or contributing visibly to machines.

## Companion effects — PROPOSED

- Ore Golem: increases extraction rate.
- Ember Sprite: lowers furnace preparation time.
- Clockwork Helper: improves smelting rate/efficiency.
- Golden Forge Dragon: dramatic premium appearance and one/two strong production bonuses.

The companion system may also affect storage and material use. Focus on stats that map to an existing gameplay purpose. Avoid building combat, pathfinding-heavy pet swarms or a duplicate RPG.

An earlier bonus illustration: basic +10%, rare +30%, legendary +60% extraction; golden dragon +100% extraction and +20% smelting. These were **samples, not approved balance**.

## Earning and hatching — PLANNED

The latest design direction **includes basic egg hatching**:
- Free opening: one egg at a time.
- Earnable companion eggs purchased with in-game progression currency.
- Paid multi-hatch convenience: 3 or 5 eggs per opening (quantities/progression to finalize).
- Players can equip a few companions. Earlier suggested starting 2 slots, earning a 3rd, but slot counts are **proposed**.
- Guaranteed premium companions sold directly, with disclosed exact bonuses, rather than relying exclusively on paid random eggs.
- Seasonal companions/eggs possible; offer earnable seasonal content as well.

The earliest brainstorming briefly favored pets obtained directly with no hatching; the later discussion explicitly shifted toward a **small egg-hatching system**. Follow the later direction while keeping it light.

## Avoid a second RNG grind

Weapon crafting is the primary excitement. Pet hatching should have modest catalogs, quick UI, clear odds where needed, automation/multi-hatch as optional conveniences, and non-paywalled useful pets. Reuse rigs/animations and recolor variants where possible.

## Weapon collection and showcase — PLANNED

Forge result can be **sold for currency** or **kept** in inventory/gallery. Show the owner's best Legendary/Mythic works in a physical workshop display, with clear title, selected material, rolled rarity, quality, trait(s) and sale record. A Masterpiece Collection and personal best weapon are core status goals; collection milestones may grant small utility bonuses/cosmetics after balancing.

Permanent collection records should not disappear on standard evolution. A sold weapon may still count for a discovery journal, without allowing resale.

No trading or theft at launch unless intentionally approved later.

## Content/asset approach

Create a small number of polished companion shapes (including signature premium creatures). Rarity variations should mostly use colors/effects. Pet mesh/animation rig reuse matters; Tripo-generated meshes may require Blender cleanup and rigging. Server ownership/pet effects must be verified rather than trusting client-reported equip stats.

# Vision and core experience

## Identity — LOCKED

**Forge Frenzy** is a Roblox blacksmithing incremental/collection game. **Blacksmithing is the player's main activity; the factory exists to supply materials**, not the other way around. The design aims for deep, numerical, repeatable progression and very little horizontal systems complexity. It is deliberately not an MMO, dungeon game or Adventurer's Rise continuation.

Inspirations discussed: Oil Empire (compact, efficient automated production); Gym League (immediate, visible progression); pet/collecting simulators (luck, collections, premium convenience, status); Fly to Space (multiple monetization paths, evolving/rebirth and public leaderboards). Use inspiration for design patterns, not for duplicating third-party assets, branding or code.

## Core player loop — LOCKED

1. Select an unlocked ore/metal from an upgraded extractor; machines generate and refine it over time.
2. Choose a **weapon type** and **material**.
3. Heat/hammer/finish the item in a short blacksmithing interaction. Clicking performance increases forge luck.
4. Reveal **craftsmanship, rarity, design, traits and final selling value**, bounded by that material's potential value range.
5. **Sell** most results for currency, or **keep/display** exceptional items.
6. Reinvest coins in extraction/refining/storage, forge gear/equipment, companions and additional production capability.
7. Reach stronger materials, collect rarer weapons, evolve/rebirth for permanent bonuses and compete on leaderboards. Repeat.

**Meaningful choices:** faster lower-price materials versus slower high-price materials; weapon class and ore selection; investment in throughput versus forge speed versus pets; sell now or keep for a showcase.

## Motivations and return-play — PLANNED

- Frequent early improvements and clear next affordance.
- Distinct material eras; higher potential weapon values without needing large maps.
- Rare roll anticipation and beautiful weapon reveals.
- Personal showcases, visible pets/machinery, and aspirational other players.
- Stored/offline-generated materials encourage a return session; capacity caps keep manual forging relevant.
- Multiple visible leaderboards and evolution ranks.
- Paid upgrades shorten time to goals, but gameplay remains meaningfully playable free.

## Scope guardrails — LOCKED philosophy

- Compact workshop plots and/or central hub; **no giant open-world quest chain**.
- No weapon combat stats/abilities unless gameplay needs them.
- No PvE combat, classes, full crafting recipes, large NPC ecosystem, or elaborate dungeons for launch.
- No automatic forging baseline that makes player participation obsolete; production automation supports the forge.
- No hundreds of hand-authored weapons or individual mines for every material.
- Prioritize forge feel, screen readability, short satisfying reveals, and reliable saving over broad features.

## Plausible first-session flow — PROPOSED, playtest

Spawn in a small workshop with one extractor and a usable furnace/anvil. The first forge attempt happens almost immediately. Sell first weapon, upgrade a machine, observe more throughput, unlock a second metal, then chase a distinctive rare result. Show the player why their next upgrade matters in-world.

Do not implement forced tutorials, spammy upsells, or arbitrary paywalls as substitutes for a rewarding free loop.

## Social setting — PLANNED

Shared hub with visible workshop plots and player showcase surfaces; players can inspect others' exceptional weapons and pets. Leaderboards physically displayed. Truly exceptional forge results may trigger occasional global or server announcements (rate-limit them). Trading, PvP, stealing and guilds are **not** approved launch commitments.

# Tripo asset manifest

**Status: PROPOSED asset plan, nothing generated or imported yet.** Last updated 2026-10-08. Machine-readable twin: [design/tripo-asset-manifest.json](../design/tripo-asset-manifest.json); keep the two in sync.

This is the official list of 3D assets Forge Frenzy should create externally in Tripo3D. Each entry says what the asset is, how important it is, how it is reused, its triangle budget and the exact prompt to give Tripo. It follows the locked asset philosophy in [docs/08](08_ASSETS_AND_TOOLING.md) and [docs/02](02_FORGING_AND_RNG.md): Common through Epic weapons share a small family of base meshes, material differences come from color, material and effects, and only Legendary/Mythic weapons and special premium items get bespoke geometry.

> Generating these with paid Tripo credits requires explicit owner authorization (see [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md)). Item names, bonuses and prices referenced here are as PROPOSED or OPEN as they are in the source docs. If an asset is missing, the game uses the listed fallback rather than blocking (see [docs/10](10_BUILD_AND_ACCEPTANCE.md)).

## Style bible

Every prompt is exactly two sentences. Sentence one describes the object; sentence two is the **shared style sentence**, copied verbatim so every model lands in the same visual family:

> Stylized fantasy-industrial blacksmithing asset for a Roblox simulator with a chunky readable silhouette, slightly exaggerated proportions, clean low-to-mid poly forms, bold hand-painted materials and subtle magical glow, no photorealism and no gritty PBR detail.

Subject-sentence conventions:

- Lead with the object type and its silhouette-defining features, then materials and one or two magical accents. Avoid scene, background, lighting or camera words.
- Recolorable assets (standard weapons, Legendary/Mythic metal, ingot, ore, smith's hammer) say **"neutral light-gray metal ... ready for in-engine recoloring"** (or neutral silver for Legendary/Mythic) so Tripo does not bake a specific metal color.
- Rigged companions end with **"standing in a neutral pose with limbs slightly apart for rigging"**.
- Premium items start with **"A premium ..."** and lean on gold, gems and stronger glow; free items stay iron, brass, wood and stone.
- Era progression for extractors follows the doc 03 palette: warm earthy, polished, dark industrial, blue-green arcane, elemental, crimson/spectral/cosmic, radiant gold and pearl, ancient otherworldly.

## Import conventions

- **Cleanup:** every Tripo output goes through Blender: remove floaters, fix normals, decimate to budget, set pivot and scale, then export FBX or OBJ for Studio import. Record origin, triangle count and texture size in the asset inventory (doc 08).
- **Textures:** 512x512 for props and weapons, 1024x1024 for machines, pets and hero assets. Recolorable assets keep a near-white or light-gray albedo so Studio tinting reads correctly.
- **Split parts:** separate any piece that animates, glows or recolors independently (drills, hammer arms, egg halves, glass, gems, metal vs grip). Each split part counts toward the asset's triangle budget.
- **Weapons:** grip center at origin, blade or head pointing +Y, edge facing +X. Scale so a one-handed sword is about 4 studs, a greatsword about 6, a dagger about 2. All Legendary/Mythic meshes of a class share that class's grip point and scale.
- **Machines:** base on the XZ plane at Y=0, front (interaction side) facing -Z, footprint inside the plot grid cell. Advanced and premium variants keep the basic version's footprint and interaction attachments.
- **Companions:** about 2 to 3 studs tall, facing -Z. Bodies that share a rig family (quadruped, biped, floating, winged) share animations.
- **Security:** these are Tripo meshes, not Creator Store models, so no scripts arrive with them; still audit any packaged model before insertion.

Not on this list on purpose: walls, floors, roofs, fences, terrain and plot layout (built from Studio parts), particles and trails (Studio VFX), and 2D UI icons.

## Suggested generation order

1. **Critical** assets, in this order: anvil, furnace, Prospector Drill extractor, smelter, storage rack, basic auto hammer, the five design-1 standard weapons, ingot, ore chunk, reveal turntable, smith's hammer, the three starter companions, forge egg, hatch station, plot sign, leaderboard board, then the Legendary and Mythic swords to prove the bespoke pipeline.
2. **Important** assets, starting with the remaining standard weapons and Legendary/Mythic weapons (most visible to players), then advanced machine looks and extractor eras 2 to 5, then hub landmarks and premium items.
3. **Nice-to-have** assets as budget allows.

After each batch: import, check triangle count and texture memory in Studio, view at phone resolution next to existing assets, and adjust the subject sentence (never the style sentence) if a result drifts off-style.

## Maintenance

- Add or change assets in the JSON and this document together; ids are stable and used by future import tooling.
- Keep prompts at two sentences and under 1,000 characters, always ending with the shared style sentence.
- When an asset is generated and imported, record it in the asset inventory with its Roblox asset id; do not mark it IMPLEMENTED here until it is in the place and verified.
